Scheduled jobs: cron#

The cron service runs commands at set times: backups at night, a report every hour, something at every boot. Each user has a crontab, a table of times and commands, and the jobs in it run as that user, in their home directory.

crontab -e        # edit yours (in le); it's checked when you save
crontab -l        # show it
crontab -r        # remove it
svc logs cron     # what ran, and what the jobs printed

The format#

# m   h   dom mon dow   command
*/5   *   *   *   *     date >> ~/heartbeat.log
0     3   *   *   1-5   rsync -a ~/notes/ /media/BACKUP/notes/
30    8   1   *   *     echo "first of the month" >> ~/reminders.txt
@reboot                 date >> ~/boots.log

The five fields are minute, hour, day of the month, month and day of the week:

FieldValues
minute0-59
hour0-23
day of month1-31
month1-12, or jan-dec
day of week0-7 (0 and 7 are Sunday), or sun-sat

Each field is * (any), a number, a list (1,15), a range (1-5) or a step (*/10, 0-30/10). When both day fields are restricted, a day matching either runs the job. @hourly, @daily, @weekly, @monthly, @yearly and @reboot (when Lepton starts) can stand in for the five fields.

Times are local, in the time zone date shows (see time): a job at 0 9 * * * runs at 9 in the morning where you are.

  • NAME=value lines set environment variables for the jobs after them.
  • In a command, % starts its standard input (write \% for a percent sign, as in date +\%F).
  • Jobs run without your ~/.zshrc, so use commands, not aliases.

The log#

What a job prints goes to the cron log, not to a terminal or mail:

svc logs cron       # recent lines
svc logs cron -f    # follow it
Nov 20 03:00:00 cron[105]: (alice) CMD (rsync -a ~/notes/ /media/BACKUP/notes/)
Nov 20 03:00:01 cron[105]: (alice) exited with status 23: rsync -a ~/notes/ /media/BACKUP/notes/

The service#

cron is on by default: it starts at boot and reads every crontab each minute, so a change takes effect at the next minute. To turn it off:

sudo svc disable --now cron

Crontabs are kept in /var/lib/lepton/cron, so on an installed system they survive restarts. Root manages anyone's with crontab -u USER.
