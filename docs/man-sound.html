sound(1)#

NAME#

sound - sound cards: list them, choose where sound plays, play a test tone

SYNOPSIS#

sound [list] | use N | use bluetooth | use auto | test

DESCRIPTION#

List the sound cards and choose which one plays sound. Sound plays on the card added last, so a USB headset or Bluetooth headphones take over when they appear and hand back when they go.

COMMANDS#

  • sound, sound list: the sound cards, with a * on the one playing sound.
  • use N: always play on card N.
  • use bluetooth: play on the Bluetooth headphones.
  • use auto: play on the card added last again.
  • test: play three notes, to check you can hear them.

SEE ALSO#

volume(1), play(1), beep(1), bluetooth(1), https://trylepton.app/docs/sound.html
