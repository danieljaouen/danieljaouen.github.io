Printing#

Lepton prints to network printers that speak IPP, the driverless way: AirPrint and IPP Everywhere printers, which is most printers made in the last ten years. Printers on your network are found by themselves; there is nothing to install.

printers                     # the printers, and choose your default
lp notes.txt                 # print (to your default printer)
lp -d office -n 2 report.pdf # to a given printer, two copies
lpstat -p -o                 # printers, and the jobs waiting
cancel office-3              # cancel a job

On the classic desktop, Start → Printers lists them and lets you choose your default. Firefox's Print dialog lists the same printers, and prints to the one you choose.

What can be printed#

PDF files, JPEG pictures and plain text (which Lepton lays out as a PDF, in Courier, on the printer's paper size: A4 or US Letter). The printer must take PDF (or JPEG, for pictures): most do. Firefox prints web pages as PDF.

Printers#

Printers found on the network (by mDNS, as AirPrint finds them) appear by themselves, named after the printer (Office_Laser), and can be used without adding them. lpinfo -v lists what is found right now.

A printer that can't be found that way (on another network, say) is added by its address, with sudo:

sudo lpadmin -p office -v ipp://192.168.1.50/ipp/print -D "Office laser" -L "2nd floor"
sudo lpadmin -d office      # the system's default
sudo lpadmin -x office      # remove it

The address is usually ipp://ADDRESS/ipp/print (ipps:// for an encrypted connection); the printer's web page or manual has it.

Your default printer is yours: printers or lpoptions -d NAME keeps it in ~/.cups/lpoptions, where Firefox (CUPS's library) reads it too. Without one, the system's default is used, or the only printer there is.

The print service#

cupsd, run by the cups service (on by default), keeps the queue and sends each job on to its printer. Like CUPS, it speaks IPP on 127.0.0.1:631, only to this machine, which is how lp and Firefox reach it. http://localhost:631/ shows the printers and jobs (in Firefox), and svc logs cups what was printed or why not.

Not supported#

USB printers, printers that take only raster images (image/pwg-raster, image/urf, no PDF), PostScript and PCL drivers, and sharing Lepton's printers with other computers.
